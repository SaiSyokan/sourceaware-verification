# Dataset Licenses and Usage Conditions

This page records the source-specific licenses and usage conditions reported for the 16 public data sources used by the work. It is provided as a provenance and redistribution checklist, not as legal advice.

A license attached to code, annotations, or metadata does **not** necessarily grant rights over underlying third-party images, news articles, or social-media content. The repository therefore distinguishes explicit dataset/repository licenses from source-specific restrictions and third-party content rights.

## Repository release policy

In constructing and releasing OctantFake-related resources, provenance of the constituent sources should be preserved and no ownership of third-party content is claimed. Redistribution of source-derived images, text, or metadata remains subject to the applicable terms of the original datasets and content providers.

Where direct redistribution is restricted, release only the annotations, identifiers, references, or other material that the applicable source terms permit. The gated OctantFake dataset card and its terms remain the authoritative release surface for OctantFake itself.

Because third-party terms can change, re-check the current upstream terms before redistribution or archival release.
